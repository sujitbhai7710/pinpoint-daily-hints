## How to Use Category Hints Effectively

Category hints in LinkedIn Pinpoint are the most important clue you get. Here's how to maximize their value:

### Step 1: Identify the Category Scope
- Broad categories (e.g., "Business") → thousands of possible words
- Narrow categories (e.g., "Financial Ratios") → much smaller word space

### Step 2: Brainstorm Related Terms
Write down 5-10 words immediately associated with the category.

### Step 3: Cross-Reference with Letter Count
The word length eliminates most brainstormed words instantly.

### Step 4: Test Common Patterns
- Business words often end in: -tion, -ment, -ance, -ity
- Tech words often end in: -ware, -ing, -tion
- Leadership words often end in: -ship, -ment
